Associate Attorney, Real Estate & Land Use

Rehoboth Beach OfficeFull-time2–5 years

Hudson, Jones, Jaywork & Fisher is seeking an associate attorney to join the real estate and land use practice in our Rehoboth Beach office.

You'll handle residential and commercial transactions across coastal Sussex County, advise property owners and developers, and appear before local planning and zoning bodies. You'll own your files from the start, with close support from partners who have practiced at the beach for decades.

Office
Rehoboth Beach
Schedule
Full-time, in office
Bar admission
Delaware

What you'll do

  • Manage residential and commercial real estate transactions from contract through settlement
  • Review title, surveys, and HOA documents, and resolve title and closing issues
  • Represent clients before Sussex County and municipal planning & zoning boards
  • Draft and negotiate purchase agreements, leases, easements, and financing documents
  • Assist with related estate planning and estate administration matters

What we're looking for

  • J.D. from an accredited law school
  • Admission to the Delaware Bar (or sitting for the next Delaware exam)
  • 2–5 years of real estate or transactional experience preferred
  • Strong drafting skills and careful attention to detail
  • Comfort working directly with clients, lenders, and realtors

What we offer

Competitive compensation; health, dental, and vision insurance; a 401(k) retirement plan; paid CLE and bar dues; and a collaborative, long-tenured team in one of Delaware's fastest-growing communities.

Associate Attorney, Litigation & Municipal Law

Dover OfficeFull-time3+ years

Hudson, Jones, Jaywork & Fisher is seeking an associate attorney to join the litigation and municipal practice in our Dover office.

Steps from the Kent County courthouse and Legislative Hall, you'll litigate civil matters across Delaware's courts and support our long-standing representation of towns and governmental entities throughout Kent County, with a primary focus on Kent and Sussex counties.

Office
Dover
Schedule
Full-time, in office
Bar admission
Delaware

What you'll do

  • Handle civil litigation from intake through trial, including motion practice and discovery
  • Appear in Superior Court, Court of Chancery, Court of Common Pleas, and JP Court
  • Advise municipal clients on FOIA, ordinances, land use, and governance issues
  • Attend council and board meetings as counsel for municipal clients
  • Support business law and landlord-tenant matters as needed

What we're looking for

  • J.D. from an accredited law school
  • Active member of the Delaware State Bar
  • 3+ years of litigation experience; judicial clerkship a plus
  • Excellent research, writing, and oral advocacy skills
  • Interest in public-sector and municipal work

What we offer

Competitive compensation; health, dental, and vision insurance; a 401(k) retirement plan; paid CLE and bar dues; and meaningful courtroom and client responsibility early in your career.
